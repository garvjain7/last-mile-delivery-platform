How a delivery works

Four steps, and you can follow every one of them.

1

Order placed

Your seller confirms the order, and we send it to the warehouse closest to you.

2

Packed and assigned

The warehouse prepares your parcel and puts it on a driver's route for the day.

3

On the way

Follow your delivery as it moves, and see how many stops are left before yours.

4

Delivered

You get a confirmation. If we couldn't reach you, we tell you what happens next.
